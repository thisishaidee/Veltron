3 · Agent management

Only the treasury owner can change the configured agent and its per-payment cap. The contract rejects zero caps and reserved account conflicts.

Connect owner and refresh verified state first.

4 · Safety reserve management

Changing the reserve changes how much of the treasury remains protected from discretionary spending. The contract enforces solvency before accepting the new reserve.

Connect owner and refresh verified state first.

5 · Emergency payment controls

Pausing blocks new outgoing treasury transfers, including agent requests and commitment settlements. Resuming removes that circuit breaker. Both operations require owner authorization.

6 · Commitment settlement center

Settlement sends the committed amount to the recipient already recorded in the commitment. On successful token callback, the contract removes the commitment. A failed transfer leaves the commitment available again.
